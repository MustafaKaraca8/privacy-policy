Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe? Yes. All your words and progress stay on your device. The app works fully offline and requires no account.

How do I restore my Pro subscription? Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases".

How do I delete my words? Open a word or list and use the delete option; everything is stored locally on your device.
