Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the My Dictionary mobile application ("the App", "we") handles user data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to operate primarily offline. Most data — including the words you save, your topics, learning progress, and game statistics — is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

The following limited data is processed when you use Pro/subscription features:

  • Anonymous user identifier: a randomly generated ID created by RevenueCat to associate purchases with your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Purchase history: the subscription or in-app product you purchased, the purchase date, and entitlement status. Payment details (credit card, etc.) are handled exclusively by Google Play and are never visible to us.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to enable subscription functionality:

  • RevenueCat — for managing subscriptions and validating purchases. RevenueCat may receive the anonymous user identifier and purchase history described above. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — for processing all payments. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How Your Data Is Used

The data described above is used solely to:

  • Unlock and manage Pro features for paying users;
  • Validate purchases and prevent fraud;
  • Restore previous purchases when you reinstall the App.

We do not use this data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any party other than the service providers listed above (RevenueCat, Google Play), and only to the extent strictly necessary to deliver the subscription functionality.

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App, RevenueCat, and Google Play is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights — Data Deletion

Since the App does not require an account and stores most data locally, uninstalling the App will remove all local data from your device. To request deletion of any anonymous purchase data held by RevenueCat, please contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
